Breakdown entries: six options

The same Spent panel each time, with your Expense category opened. Rupee entries are shown in AED first at the account's rate (0.01329), with the original underneath where it applies.

1 · Thread Live now

Entries hang off the row on a thin rule in the panel's colour. The quietest option, and it keeps the panel short.

2 · Timeline

Each entry sits on its day. Good for spotting when the money went, e.g. two SC payments weeks apart.

3 · Split bar

The category's bar breaks into one segment per entry, with matching swatches and shares. You see straight away that one entry is most of it.

4 · By person

Entries grouped under who spent them, each with a subtotal. Handy for a shared household.

5 · Inset cards

Each entry is its own small rounded card, with a currency tag on foreign ones. Matches the rounded look and is the easiest to tap on a phone.

6 · Mini ledger

A small table with a column each for date, who, AED and the original. Densest, and the easiest to scan by column.
